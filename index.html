Tracker definitions and symbols
Latest NAV
Net asset value per share on the date shown, read automatically from the fund company or a price-data site (linked in Fund detail). Funds price on different days, so dates differ. "Not updated" means the last check couldn't read a new price, so the previous one is shown.
YTD, 1 yr, 5 yr p.a.
Total returns from the fund menu review, as of the date it gives for each fund (mostly 30 Sep 2026). 5 yr is annualised.
AI exposure
How much the fund relies on AI, chip and data-centre stocks, from None to Very high.
Est. AI-crash move
The review's estimate of how far the fund could fall in a sharp AI-led sell-off; the table shows the central estimate.
Other risks
Non-AI threats in the news (rates, oil, currencies, Taiwan, French debt), rated Low, Medium or High.
Overall risk
A combined judgement of AI exposure, other risks and volatility on a five-step scale from Very low to Very high, viewed from a USD-based investor.
Volatility 1–7
An SRI-style rating of how much the price swings.
(e)
Estimate from published NAVs or a sister share class.
*
Institutional class of the same fund.
**
R2 USD class.
†
US-listed twin fund.
‡
Master fund the feeder invests in.
n/p
No current public figure; check with the provider.
